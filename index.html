Step 2: Build Achievement 1

Answer these questions to build a strong, regulation-compliant achievement

Where/when did this happen? Keep it brief - just the setting.
Good examples: "as Platoon Sergeant during deployment to Kuwait" or "while serving as Company Executive Officer" or "across three subordinate battalions"
Write complete sentences. Start with a strong verb and describe what they actually did.
Good examples: "Led squad-level training that improved readiness" or "Coordinated logistics support across multiple locations" or "Managed personnel accountability for 200 Soldiers"
Give specific numbers, percentages, quantities. Be concrete and factual.
Good examples: "improved readiness from 70% to 92%" or "trained 150 Soldiers with 95% pass rate" or "managed $2.5M in equipment with zero losses"
Why did this matter? Connect to readiness, mission capability, or unit effectiveness.
Good examples: "strengthened unit deployment readiness" or "improved operational effectiveness" or "enhanced mission capability across the formation"
